Welcome to RockingShip, a harbour for out-of-the-box thinking.

The projects moored here look unrelated: a logic engine, a curve editor, a video transport, a number format, a compiler.
They are one idea, met in different materials: **keep the essence of information, and let the rest grow from it.**

Woven together, they form one line.
`untangle`, the centre project, holds logic as structure.
`ccbc` faces the continuous world, where a form is detected at points and rebuilt from them.
`armonika` and `xtools` face the machine, where programs become streams of objects of exactly their own size.
`jsFractalZoom` and `splash` are how it is seen and sent.

The cards below start at the centre and work outward. The research itself is presented at [rockingship.org](https://rockingship.org).
